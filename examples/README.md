## `greedyQSimple`

[User Experience Survey](./simple-satisfaction-study/README.md) asks adults who have used greedyQ about satisfaction, usability, problems, and future use. It demonstrates a nonexperimental direct-link survey with consent, eligibility, conditional follow-ups, withdrawal, descriptive analysis, and deployment artifacts.

## `greedyQExperiment`

[Digital Service Information Study](./complete-study/README.md) tests how two randomized presentations of the same fictional policy information affect support. It demonstrates persistent assignment, condition-specific stimuli, quality checks, Prolific integration, preregistration, and native surveydown export.

Every reference is fictional and blocked from real fielding until its placeholders, governance details, hands-on preview review, and live integrations are replaced and verified.
