# Digital Service Information Study

[한국어](./study-plan(kor).md)

## What this study asks

Does presenting the same digital-service policy information in a benefit-focused or cost-focused frame change adults’ support for the policy?

## Who participates

Adults aged 18 or older who are recruited through Prolific and meet the study’s stated eligibility requirements.

## What participants experience

Participants read the consent page, confirm eligibility, answer baseline questions, and are assigned to one of two information presentations. They then answer comprehension, policy-support, attention, and demographic questions. They may decline, be screened out, withdraw, or complete the study.

## Main comparison

The study compares post-presentation policy support between the two assigned framing conditions while following the approved analysis plan.

## What is ready to review

The complete participant flow can be reviewed in `preview.html`, including both conditions and all endings. The consent language, survey questions, analysis plan, and preregistration draft are also available in this folder.

## What is not yet complete

This is a fictional reference study. It has not been submitted to OSF, deployed to Vercel or Supabase, connected to a live Prolific study, or approved for real participant recruitment. Real contributor information and institutional details remain intentionally unresolved.
