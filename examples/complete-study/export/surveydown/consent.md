## Study purpose

This fictional study examines how descriptions of a hypothetical municipal digital-assistance program affect opinions about the program.

## What you will do

You will answer background questions, read one short program description selected at random, and answer questions about it. Participation should take about five minutes.

## Risks and benefits

The study is designed to present no more than minimal risk. You may skip optional questions or stop at any time. There is no guaranteed direct benefit.

## Data and confidentiality

The study records your panel participant identifier, responses, assigned condition, consent version, and operational timestamps. It does not collect your name, IP address, or browser fingerprint by default. The fictional research team controls the study database.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. Refusing or stopping does not create a penalty from the research team. Use the withdrawal option during the study to stop. The deletion-request option records a request for the research team; it does not claim that deletion has already occurred.
