# greedyQ User Experience Survey

[한국어](./study-plan(kor).md)

## What this survey asks

How satisfied are people who have used greedyQ, what parts of the experience work well, and what should be improved first?

## Who participates

Adults who have used greedyQ at least once to create or revise a questionnaire.

## What participants experience

Participants read the consent information, confirm that they are eligible, describe how they used greedyQ, rate their experience, report any important problem, and optionally suggest improvements. They may decline, be screened out, withdraw, or submit their feedback.

## Main result

The primary result is the distribution and mean of overall satisfaction on the seven-point scale. The survey also summarizes ease of starting, clarity of the AI conversation, usefulness of the preview, confidence in the result, time saved, problems encountered, likelihood of recommending greedyQ, and intention to use it again.

## Privacy choice

The survey intentionally does not request names, email addresses, institutions, credentials, or private research details. Open-text questions remind participants not to include them.

## Current status

This is a fictional golden-reference survey. Its participant flow can be reviewed in `preview.html`. It has not been reviewed by a real institution, deployed to production, or opened for responses.
