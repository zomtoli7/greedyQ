## Main summary

Report the number of eligible submitted responses, the full distribution of `overall_satisfaction`, and its mean with a two-sided 95% confidence interval. Do not treat the sample as representative of all researchers unless the recruitment process supports that claim.

## Additional summaries

- Report every item in `experience_ratings` separately; do not combine them into a scale without additional validation.
- Report the distributions of `confidence`, `time_saved`, `completion_status`, `recommend_score`, and `use_again`.
- For descriptive product tracking, calculate the conventional Net Promoter Score categories from `recommend_score`: detractors 0–6, passives 7–8, and promoters 9–10. Report the category counts with the score.
- Summarize `had_problem` and `problem_area`. Review open text only after removing accidental identifying or confidential content.
- Compare results by use frequency, main use, AI product, completion status, and guide-access method only as exploratory descriptions. Show group sizes and avoid causal language.

## Missing responses and exclusions

Describe item-level missingness. The primary summary includes eligible, consented sessions that reached the completed outcome. Consent refusals, screened-out sessions, withdrawals, previews, and test sessions are not product-feedback responses. Do not exclude completed responses merely because they are unfavorable.
