# Governance and IRB Module

[한국어](./guide(kor).md)

Ask whether detailed governance and IRB work should happen now or after the coherent instrument draft. Deferral changes timing, not whether the work is required. Immediately screen for vulnerable populations, sensitive or identifying data, deception, elevated risk, regulated interventions, and known institutional restrictions.

Record the institution, protocol title or identifier when known, review status, investigator contact, risk description, compensation, data handling, complaints contact, and unresolved requirements. Never state or imply that a study has IRB approval without researcher-provided and verifiable evidence. greedyQ output is drafting assistance, not institutional approval or legal advice.
