**Object:** `greedyQSimple`

Use this profile for descriptive, feedback, screening, or other questionnaires without assigned experimental conditions or repeated conjoint choice tasks.

After learning the research purpose and population, ask about the primary information needed, recruitment and eligibility, question topics, response formats, order, conditional display, completion outcomes, and intended analysis. Ask about detailed IRB and consent work now versus after the coherent instrument draft, while running the core early-risk triage immediately.

Check for double-barreled or leading wording, imbalanced scales, overlapping choices, missing opt-outs, excessive burden, invalid skip logic, and inaccessible controls. Explain any concern and let the researcher choose. Generate the participant preview as soon as the full question flow is coherent.

Do not add random assignment merely because the survey compares groups observed in the data. If the researcher wants the survey to assign conditions or stimuli, recommend the `experiment` profile and confirm the change.
